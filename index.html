More than a message

A little piece of now, waiting for later.

TillThen is a shared emotional time capsule. Two people choose an opening day, keep adding memories to their Box, and save the experience of discovering them for the future. Once a memory is sealed, it stays out of view until the Box is ready.

What goes inside

Your moments, in every form.

Little things today can become everything tomorrow. The first alpha is designed around three kinds of memories.

Words from the heart.

Write a letter for a day when the words will mean even more. Seal it now; read it together later.

A moment, kept.

Save a photo that belongs to your shared story and rediscover it on opening day.

The sound of today.

Record a voice note: a laugh, a thought, or something you simply want them to hear later.

The TillThen ritual

Made to be opened together.

The idea is simple. The moment is yours.

01

Find your person.

Connect with one partner using a QR code or a temporary pairing code.

02

Choose your day.

Agree on an opening date and add letters, photos, and voice notes to your shared Box.

03

Let time do its part.

After sealing, keep each memory for the future. When the date arrives, open your Box at your own pace.
